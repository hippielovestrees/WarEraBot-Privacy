# WarEraBot Privacy Policy

WarEraBot is operated by HippieLovesTrees. This policy covers WarEraBot’s registration, verification, and server-management features.

## What WarEraBot stores

- **Discord membership and access.** The bot may store your Discord user ID, the Discord server ID, your membership and registration status, and records of roles, channel permissions, or nicknames the bot manages. This lets it manage access before and after verification.
- **WarEra verification.** If you start verification, the bot records the selected WarEra player ID and the details needed to check the ownership challenge, such as a company verification code or a donation transaction reference. Successful verification links your Discord ID to your WarEra player ID.
- **Game information.** The bot obtains WarEra information such as your player name, citizenship, level, activity status, build, and government, party, or military unit membership from WarEra and, where configured, a public game-data feed. It uses this information to apply server rules and keep roles and access current.
- **Operations and history.** The bot keeps records of verification, administrative changes, access it grants, and sync activity. Some operational records may include identifiers needed to resolve problems.

We use this information to verify accounts, manage access and roles, and operate and troubleshoot the bot. Where UK data protection law applies, our legitimate interests are running the server’s verification and access controls and maintaining the bot. Partner sharing requires your separate choice.

## Optional sharing with Tommybot

If the Tommybot connection is enabled and your WarEra profile shows UK citizenship, you can choose to let Tommybot look up the WarEra player ID linked to your Discord account. This lets Tommybot recognise your registration for commands such as `/me` without a second registration. Sharing starts off. Choose **Share with Tommybot** to enable it or use `/connections` to stop future lookups. Tommybot is operated separately. If you ask us to delete data we shared with Tommybot, we will coordinate with its operator about any copies they may hold.

## Unlinking, leaving and deleting data

- **`/unlink`** removes the account link, cancels pending verification, and stops future sharing through that link. The bot’s access sync handles automatic roles and channel permissions. Some verification and audit records remain until cleanup or deletion.
- **`/privacy opt-out`** requests deletion of bot-managed records for this server. It uses a Discord role called **Manual** to keep routine bot automation from recreating those records. The bot removes access it granted, and staff can manage any remaining access in Discord. You can use `/privacy opt-in` to resume using the bot and verify again.
- **Leaving** triggers cleanup of the bot’s records for that server once the bot confirms the departure. If you still use the bot in another server sharing its database, your registration there can continue. The bot can retain independently collected WarEra game data without the Discord account link.

We retain operational data while needed for verification, server access, or troubleshooting. Deletion requests also cover relevant logs, exports, or backups if they exist. Backup data may remain until those copies expire, and deleted records will be removed again if a backup is restored.

## Contact and your choices

Send a private Discord message request to **hippielovestrees** to ask what data we hold, correct it, request deletion, or raise a privacy concern. Start your message with **WarEraBot privacy request** and briefly say what you need.

If you've left the server and Discord won't deliver your message, you can join the [public WarEra Discord](https://discord.com/invite/war-era-1102968781699043428) to find our profile and send a private message. Keep the details of your request in the private message. We may ask you to confirm control of the Discord account so we apply your request to the right records.

If UK data protection law applies, you can also [raise a concern with the Information Commissioner’s Office](https://ico.org.uk/make-a-complaint/).
